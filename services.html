Services

Repairs and installations

The services we undertake are set out below, each with its usual scope of works. Every service carries a three-month warranty.

Refrigeration

Repairs to fridges, fridge-freezers and freezers.

Fridge repair

Fridges and fridge-freezers that are not cooling, are icing up or are running constantly, diagnosed and repaired.

Freezer repair

Chest and upright freezers repaired, including thermostat, fan and defrost faults.

Regassing and leak repair

Refrigerant leaks located and sealed, and the system evacuated, recharged and tested.

Refrigeration maintenance

Condensers cleaned, seals checked and running temperatures recorded, to keep units in service.

Air conditioning

Installation, servicing and repair of air conditioning units.

Installation

Split units mounted and connected, with pipework, drainage and electrical connections completed and the system commissioned.

Servicing

Filters and coils cleaned, drain lines cleared and the unit checked for correct operation.

Fault finding and repair

Units that are not cooling, are leaking water or have stopped, diagnosed and repaired.

Regassing

Refrigerant levels checked and topped up once any leak has been located and repaired.

Appliances & vehicles

Household appliance repairs, and air conditioning for vehicles.

Washing machine repair

Drainage, drum, door-lock and motor faults repaired on top-loading and front-loading machines.

Home appliance repair

Repairs to other household appliances, with the fault diagnosed before parts are ordered.

Vehicle aircon regas

Vehicle air conditioning recharged with refrigerant once the system has been checked for leaks.

Vehicle aircon cleaning

The system cleaned and a performance check carried out, with outlet temperatures recorded.

Process

How a repair is carried out

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Contact us by telephone or WhatsApp with a description of the appliance or unit and the fault.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis

    We inspect the appliance or unit and identify the cause of the fault.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    We set out the repair required, the parts and the labour before work begins.

  4. 04

    Repair & warranty

    We carry out the repair and test the appliance. The work is covered by a three-month warranty.
